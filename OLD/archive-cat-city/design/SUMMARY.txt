OPTION A: DISCOVERY-FIRST ★ RECOMMENDED
  • Type: Emergent gameplay
  • Emphasis: Player agency, organic exploration
  • Story Structure: Emerges naturally through discovery
  • Gating: Soft gates (suggestions, not barriers)
  • Appeal: Explorers (★★★★★), Completionists (★★★★★), Replayable
  • Pacing: 60-80 hours for full completion
  • Strengths: Natural progression, high agency, emergent gameplay
  • Implementation: Low-medium complexity

OPTION B: STORY-DRIVEN NARRATIVE
  • Type: Linear narrative
  • Emphasis: Structured 4-act story arc
  • Story Structure: Predetermined with cinematic moments
  • Gating: Hard gates enforce progression order
  • Appeal: Casual (★★★★★), Storytellers (★★★★★), First-timers
  • Pacing: 20-30 hours main story
  • Strengths: Clear narrative, easy onboarding, emotional impact
  • Implementation: High complexity (narrative systems)

OPTION C: CHALLENGE-BASED PROGRESSION
  • Type: Mastery-driven
  • Emphasis: Skill expression, self-imposed challenges
  • Story Structure: Minimal, mechanics-focused
  • Gating: Difficulty-based unlocks
  • Appeal: Hardcore (★★★★★), Speedrunners (★★★★★), Competitive
  • Pacing: 25-50+ hours + infinite endgame
  • Strengths: High replayability, mastery tracking, competitive
  • Implementation: Medium-high complexity (balancing difficult)

HYBRID RECOMMENDATION: A+B+C
  • Hours 0-10 (Early): Story framework (B) for onboarding
  • Hours 10-35 (Mid): Discovery mechanics (A) for exploration
  • Hours 35+ (Late): Challenge tiers (C) for mastery
  • Result: Universal appeal across all player types
