PROGRESSION PHILOSOPHY
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Core Principle: Players progress by EXPLORING, DISCOVERING, and BUILDING RELATIONSHIPS
              No grinding, no time-gates, organic difficulty scaling
              Content unlocks naturally through gameplay actions

RANK PROGRESSION OVERVIEW
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

RANK 1: KITTEN (Starting Rank)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Unlock Condition: Game start
  Duration: ~15-20 minutes
  Key Activities: 
    • Learn basic controls (walk, jump, meow)
    • Explore starting area (home & garden)
    • Collect first 5-10 items
    • Meet 1-2 friendly NPCs

  Unlocked Abilities:
    ✓ Walk (3.5 m/s baseline)
    ✓ Jump v1 (1.5m height)
    ✓ Basic meow (30m range)
    ✓ Stalk mode (sneaking)
    ✓ Sprint (8 m/s, 3 sec duration)
    ✓ Basic climbing (3m height limit)

  Accessible Areas:
    📍 Home area (your starting house)
    📍 Front garden/yard
    📍 Adjacent neighbor yards (open areas)

  NPCs Met:
    🐱 Whiskers (friendly neighbor cat)
    🐱 Shadow (aloof cat - watches from distance)
    👤 Mrs. Chen (kind human neighbor)

  Story Moments:
    • Wake up at home
    • Discover the neighborhood exists
    • First encounter with another cat
    • Learn that exploration leads to discovery


RANK 2: APPRENTICE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Unlock Condition: Explore 5 locations + Meet 1 NPC at friendship level 1-2
  Duration: ~20-30 minutes gameplay time
  Key Activities:
    • Explore middle-distance areas (park, residential streets)
    • Build first relationship to level 2 with one cat
    • Collect 15-20 items (50 points collectibles)
    • Successfully complete first challenge (jump over obstacle)

  Unlocked Abilities:
    ✓ Enhanced jump (1.8m height +0.3m)
    ✓ Climbing unlock (5m height)
    ✓ Meow amplification (50m range, more attractive)
    ✓ Efficient grooming (+5% health restore)

  Accessible Areas:
    📍 Public park (playground, pond area)
    📍 Residential avenue (tree-lined street)
    📍 Backyard gardens (connected properties)
    📍 Small commercial area (cat-safe parts)

  NPCs Met & Relationships:
    🐱 Whiskers → Level 2 (close friendship)
    🐱 Shadow → Level 1 (knows you now)
    🐱 Patches (mischievous cat - first encounter)
    👤 Tommy (young boy - wants to pet)
    👤 Mr. Tanaka (gardener - sometimes feeds cats)

  Story Moments:
    • Reach the park for first time (sense of freedom/discovery)
    • Witness first Whiskers greeting (relationship feels real)
    • Discover hidden food source (garden with fish pond)
    • Meet Patches and experience personality difference


RANK 3: ADEPT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Unlock Condition: 
    - Explore 15+ locations 
    - Befriend 2+ NPCs to level 2-3
    - Collect 30+ items
  Duration: ~30-40 minutes gameplay time
  Key Activities:
    • Venture into downtown/commercial areas
    • Build deep relationship (level 3) with 2+ cats
    • Accomplish parkour challenges (wall jump, multi-jump)
    • Discover first secret area (hidden from main streets)

  Unlocked Abilities:
    ✓ Wall jump (bonus 0.3m on vertical surfaces)
    ✓ Advanced climbing (6m height, smoother movement)
    ✓ Power meow (commanding voice, NPCs respond urgently)
    ✓ Object physics upgrade (knock heavier objects)
    ✓ Extended sprint (4 seconds instead of 3)

  Accessible Areas:
    📍 Downtown district (shops, crowds, busy streets)
    📍 Rooftop pathways (over low buildings)
    📍 Fountain plaza (central gathering point)
    📍 School grounds (playground, fields)
    📍 SECRET: Abandoned greenhouse (found behind old barn)

  NPCs Met & Relationships:
    🐱 Whiskers → Level 3 (close friend, follows you)
    🐱 Patches → Level 2-3 (playful, steals items)
    🐱 Luna (wise elder cat - knows area history)
    🐱 Tiger (territorial male - respects you now)
    👤 Ms. Lee (elderly woman - feeds cats regularly)

  Story Moments:
    • First time downtown (overwhelming sensations)
    • Learning rooftop navigation (new freedom)
    • Luna shares memory of area's past
    • Witness conflict between Patches and Tiger (choose side)
    • Find mysterious old collar in greenhouse


RANK 4: SKILLED
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Unlock Condition:
    - Explore 25+ locations
    - Befriend 3+ NPCs to level 3+
    - Collect 50+ items
    - Complete 3+ parkour challenges
  Duration: ~40-50 minutes gameplay time
  Key Activities:
    • Explore industrial areas and waterfront
    • Max friendship (level 4) with 2+ cats
    • Master advanced parkour sequences
    • Solve location-based puzzles (reach hidden areas)

  Unlocked Abilities:
    ✓ Jump v3 (2.2m height, maximum standard jump)
    ✓ Expert climbing (8m height, faster climb speed)
    ✓ Acrobatic combo (chain 3+ jumps without landing)
    ✓ Sprint boost (9.5 m/s speed)
    ✓ Precision striking (knock objects to exact location)
    ✓ Zen grooming (meditation mode for stress relief)

  Accessible Areas:
    📍 Industrial zone (warehouses, machinery, complexity)
    📍 Docks/waterfront (boats, water, new perspective)
    📍 Rooftop highway (elevated shortcuts across city)
    📍 Narrow alleyways (skill-required shortcuts)
    📍 SECRET #2: Underground tunnels (beneath city)
    📍 SECRET #3: Cargo ship in dock (interior exploration)

  NPCs Met & Relationships:
    🐱 Whiskers → Level 4 (best friend, extremely loyal)
    🐱 Patches → Level 4 (trusts you completely)
    🐱 Luna → Level 3-4 (shares deep stories)
    🐱 Tiger → Level 3 (respects your skill)
    🐱 Mochi (playful kitten - looks up to you)
    👤 Captain (old fisherman - gives stories)
    👤 Mei (artist - sketches the cats)

  Story Moments:
    • Industrial zone feels dangerous but navigable
    • Discover underground tunnel network (major revelation)
    • Patches and Tiger resolve conflict with your help
    • Luna reveals the "truth" about the neighborhood
    • Meet Mochi and feel protective/nurturing
    • Capture moment: sunset on rooftop with Whiskers

    🐱 Whiskers → Level 5 (SOUL BONDED - unconditional bond)
    🐱 Patches → Level 5 (sees you as family)
    🐱 Luna → Level 5 (shares final wisdom)
    🐱 Tiger → Level 4-5 (respects your mastery)
    🐱 Mochi → Level 4 (nearly adult, you're mentor)
    🐱 Sphinx (mysterious cat - reveals themselves now)
    👤 Grandmaster Chen (mysterious visitor)

  Story Moments:
    • Lighthouse climb (physical and emotional journey)

KEY PROGRESSION DESIGN PRINCIPLES
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. NO GRINDING: Progress comes from exploration, not time spent
2. ORGANIC PACING: Every 15 min = new discovery
3. EARNED PROGRESSION: Demonstrate skill → Unlock ability
4. RELATIONSHIP MATTERS: NPC bonds unlock content + story
5. OPTIONAL DEPTH: Can ignore cosmetics/secrets, can go deep
6. NATURAL DIFFICULTY: Better areas = harder platforming
7. IMMERSION FIRST: Progression serves story, not vice versa
8. MULTIPLE PATHS: Different exploration orders work
9. DISCOVERY REWARDS: Finding secrets feels special
10. LEGACY CHOICE: Your impact on world is memorable
