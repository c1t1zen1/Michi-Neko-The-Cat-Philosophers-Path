## NEIGHBORHOOD 1: RESIDENTIAL DISTRICT (NW) — "Whisker Lane"

### 1.1 Overall Character
- **Vibe:** Cozy, intimate, nostalgic, walkable
- **Population:** 8,000-12,000 (peak hours)
- **Time Peak:** Morning (7-9 AM), Evening (5-7 PM)
- **Architecture Era:** Mixed Victorian (1890s) + Modern (1980s-2020s)
- **Primary Colors:** Warm browns, cream, sage green, brick red

### 1.2 Geography & Layout

**Block Structure:**
- Grid pattern with 80m × 80m blocks
- Narrow streets (6-8m wide) with parked cars
- Sidewalks 2-3m wide with street trees
- Alleyways connecting blocks (4-5m wide) — cat highways!

**Elevation:**
- Gentle rolling hills (±20m variation)
- Creates natural valleys with waterflows
- Southeast slope toward commercial district
- Elevated western edge (scenic overlook point)

**Key Streets:**
1. **Whisker Lane (Main):** East-west arterial, broadest street (12m)
   - Vendor stalls, markets, small cafes
   - Tree-lined with mature sycamores
   - 2-3 story mixed-use buildings

2. **Paw Avenue:** North-south spine, connects to downtown
   - Narrower (8m), more residential
   - Higher traffic during commute hours

3. **Alley Network:** Dense interconnecting small streets
   - Named alleys: "Silk Alley," "Whisker Run," "Feline Court"
   - Shortcuts through residential core
   - Opportunity for parkour routes

### 1.3 Building Typology

#### Type A: Victorian Brownstones (30% of structures)
**Characteristics:**
- 3-4 stories (12-15m height)
- Red/brown brick facade
- White stone trim, detailed cornices
- Ground-floor storefronts (small shops)
- Basement level windows (half-height)
- Steep rooflines with dormers
- Fire escapes on side/rear facades

**Interior Layout:**
- Storefronts: 5-8m wide, 3m deep
- 3-4 apartments per floor
- Narrow hallways, high ceilings (3.5m)
- Roof access via internal stairs

**Variations:** 6 facade styles (window patterns, cornice details)

#### Type B: Modern Apartments (40% of structures)
**Characteristics:**
- 5-8 stories (18-25m height)
- Concrete/glass/brick mixed
- Horizontal window banding
- Minimal ornamentation
- Ground-floor retail/cafe
- Internal courtyards
- Central elevator/stair core

**Interior Layout:**
- Modern open-plan apartments
- 1-3 bedroom units
- Shared courtyard spaces
- Rooftop access (community terraces)

**Variations:** 8 facade treatments (glass/concrete ratio, window placement)

#### Type C: Single-Family Homes (20% of structures)
**Characteristics:**
- 2-3 stories (8-12m height)
- Various architectural styles
- Small front yards
- Detached garages
- Pedestrian-scaled

**Variations:** 6 distinct house styles (Craftsman, Cape Cod, Victorian, Modern, etc.)

#### Type D: Corner Buildings (10% of structures)
**Special:** Extra tall (6-8 stories), distinctive architectural feature
**Purpose:** Landmark orientation points
**Count:** ~15 major corner buildings defining district grid

### 1.4 Points of Interest

#### **Whisker Lane Market** (Landmark)
**Location:** Central neighborhood crossroads
**Scale:** 300m × 80m linear market street
**Features:**
- Vendor stalls (20-30 pushcarts)
- Small permanent storefronts
- Outdoor seating areas
- Fountain at center
- Street performers (buskers)
- Open-air cafe tables

**NPCs:** Vendors, shoppers, tourists, street musicians
**Times:** 8 AM - 6 PM daily
**Rewards:** Can buy items here, NPC interactions

#### **Rooftop Garden Complex** (Exploration)
**Location:** Northeast corner of district
**Scale:** Network of connected rooftops across 4 buildings
**Features:**
- Elevated garden pathways (3m above street)
- Planters with flowers/vegetables
- Wooden bridges between buildings
- Central gazebo
- Water fountains
- Hidden seating nooks

**Access:** Fire escapes from street, internal building stairs
**Rewards:** Scenic views, hidden cat statue, peaceful ambiance
**NPCs:** Few (occasional gardeners, relaxing residents)

#### **Silk Alley Cat Cafe** (Special)
**Location:** Hidden in dense alley network
**Scale:** Intimate 40-seat cafe
**Features:**
- Glass storefront on narrow alley
- Interior: cushioned seating, warm lighting
- Rooftop extension with plants
- Multiple cat decorations/art
- Menu board outside

**Access:** Can enter (interior scene) or observe through window
**NPCs:** Cafe owner, customers, sitting cats
**Time:** 9 AM - 9 PM
**Reward:** Ambiance, cat characters, lore dialogue

#### **Underground Archive** (Exploration)
**Location:** Beneath old library building
**Scale:** Large basement level (100m × 60m)
**Features:**
- Vaulted brick ceiling
- Metal shelving with historical records
- Water damage, aged aesthetics
- Central reading room
- Secret passages (locked doors)
- Flooding in corner sections

**Access:** Basement stairs from library, or underground tunnel access
**Hazards:** Water hazards, unstable shelving (climbable)
**Rewards:** Rare documents, hidden history items, secret map clues
**NPCs:** Occasional librarian ghost (lore character)

#### **Rooftop Garden Overlook** (Scenic)
**Location:** Highest point, western edge
**Scale:** Small plaza (50m × 40m)
**Features:**
- 360° views of district and beyond
- Sitting stones arranged in circle
- Potted plants
- Clear sightlines to downtown
- Sunset viewing spot

**NPCs:** Few (evening visitors, loners)
**Time:** Best at golden hour (5-7 PM)
**Reward:** Mapping location, scenic photo op, NPC interactions

#### **The Courtyard** (Social Hub)
**Location:** Central district intersection
**Scale:** Large open square (150m × 150m)
**Features:**
- Cobblestone plaza
- Central fountain
- Bench seating (20+ benches)
- Playground equipment
- Community bulletin board
- Market stalls (occasional)

**NPCs:** High density (families, elderly, students)
**Peak:** Weekday afternoons, weekend mornings
**Reward:** Social observation, NPC interactions, community lore

### 1.5 Street Furniture & Details

**Lighting:**
- Traditional street lamps (every 20m)
- Warm color temperature (2700K)
- Casts down-light creating safe paths
- Some fixtures broken/flickering (atmospheric)

**Vehicles:**
- Parked sedans (50% of street edges)
- Some delivery vans
- Occasional taxis
- Moving traffic: 20-30 vehicles/minute peak hours

**Pedestrian Features:**
- Fire hydrants (every 50m) — climbable!
- Mailboxes (building-integrated)
- Trash cans (20 variations)
- Bike racks
- Bus stops with shelters

**Greenery:**
- Mature street trees (elm, sycamore, oak)
- Hanging flower baskets on lampposts
- Window planters (almost every building)
- Ivy climbing walls (accent locations)

**Signs & Text:**
- Building address numbers (bronze plaques)
- Street name signs (every intersection)
- Shop window signage (hand-painted styles)
- Graffiti (artist-quality, not vandalism)

### 1.6 Time-of-Day Activities

**Early Morning (6-8 AM):**
- Few NPCs (newspaper vendors, joggers)
- Peaceful ambiance
- Soft blue-golden lighting
- Shop gates opening
- Delivery trucks arrive

**Morning Rush (8-9 AM):**
- Moderate pedestrian traffic
- Commuters to downtown
- Kids going to school
- Cafe opening crowds

**Daytime (9 AM-5 PM):**
- Peak market activity
- Shoppers, tourists, students
- Midday worker breaks
- Bright sunlight
- Lunch crowds in cafes

**Evening Rush (5-7 PM):**
- Heavy pedestrian traffic
- Workers returning
- Street markets most active
- Golden hour lighting
- Cafe seating full

**Evening (7-9 PM):**
- Moderate activity declining
- Outdoor diners
- Street performers
- Neon signs glowing
- Families indoors

**Night (9 PM-6 AM):**
- Few NPCs (night shift, insomniacs)
- Streetlights primary illumination
- Quiet ambiance
- Some building lights on
- Eerie but safe feeling

### 1.7 Weather Effects

**Clear Days:**
- Sharp shadows in alleyways
- High visibility (good for navigation)
- Glare on glass storefronts
- Dust visible in sunbeams

**Light Rain:**
- Wet pavement reflections
- Puddles in alley depressions
- NPCs with umbrellas
- Fewer outdoor shoppers
- Gutters flowing
- Muffled ambiance

**Heavy Rain:**
- Flooding in lowest-elevation alleys
- Most shops closed
- Streets nearly empty
- Thunder echoing between buildings
- Water damage visible on lower facades

### 1.8 Vertical Exploration Routes

**Rooftop Network:**
- Fire escapes on building rears
- Connected rooftops via bridges (certain buildings)
- Water tank obstacles
- Antenna navigation challenges
- Reward: Shortcut to downtown, scenic viewing

**Underground Passages:**
- Basement access via library
- Connecting tunnel to commercial district
- Storm drains (lower sections)
- Maintenance corridors (locked)
- Reward: Dry weather shortcut, hidden items

## NEIGHBORHOOD 2: COMMERCIAL DISTRICT (SE) — "Downtown Metropolis"

### 2.1 Overall Character
- **Vibe:** Bustling, energetic, vertical, modern
- **Population:** 15,000-30,000 (peak hours)
- **Time Peak:** 9 AM - 6 PM (business hours)
- **Architecture Era:** Modern (1950s-2020s)
- **Primary Colors:** Cool grays, blues, chrome, neon accents

### 2.2 Geography & Layout

**Block Structure:**
- Regular grid (120m × 120m blocks)
- Wide streets (12-16m) with traffic lanes
- Wide sidewalks (4-5m)
- Underground infrastructure (subway, utilities)

**Elevation:**
- Relatively flat (±10m variation)
- Slight slope toward park district
- River passes through southern edge

**Key Streets:**
1. **Main Avenue:** East-west commercial spine
   - Widest boulevard (16m)
   - 6-lane traffic
   - Multiple crosswalks (zebra patterns)
   - Central median with trees

2. **Wall Street:** North-south financial corridor
   - Skyscraper canyon
   - Narrow shadows (morning/evening only)
   - Dense foot traffic
   - Underground subway access

3. **River Walk:** Waterfront promenade
   - Linear park-like street
   - Café seating overlooking water
   - Pedestrian-only sections
   - Bridge crossings

### 2.3 Building Typology

#### Type A: Skyscraper Towers (20% of structures)
**Characteristics:**
- 20-50 stories (80-200m height)
- Modern steel/glass construction
- Reflective facades
- Minimal ornamentation
- Large footprints (40m × 40m typical)
- Internal atriums

**Features:**
- Multiple elevator banks
- Ground-floor retail/restaurants
- Sky bridges connecting towers
- Rooftop terraces/helipads
- Dramatic vertical prominence

**Variations:** 12 distinct tower designs (glass ratios, colors, roof shapes)

#### Type B: Mid-Rise Office (40% of structures)
**Characteristics:**
- 8-15 stories (30-60m height)
- Mixed concrete/glass
- Horizontal banding
- Efficient floor plans
- Corner windows (offices)
- Parking structure integrated

**Interior:**
- Open office plans
- Conference rooms
- Elevator lobbies with cafes
- Rooftop access (security restricted)

**Variations:** 10 facade styles

#### Type C: Department Store (10% of structures)
**Special:** Landmark scale anchor buildings
**Characteristics:**
- 6-10 stories (25-40m height)
- Large footprint (80m × 60m typical)
- Grand entrance atrium
- Interior galleries
- Multiple levels
- Rooftop parking

**Features:**
- Domed/skylighted atriums
- Escalators (visible through windows)
- Window displays (seasonal)
- Parking garage interior (explorable)
- Multiple floor levels

#### Type D: Specialty Buildings (30% of structures)
**Types:**
- Hotels (10 stories, 100+ rooms)
- Theaters (6 stories, grand lobbies)
- Museums (8 stories, public atriums)
- Transit stations (multiple levels)
- Banks (5 stories, secure design)

### 2.4 Points of Interest

#### **Grand Central Station** (Landmark)
**Location:** District center
**Scale:** Major transportation hub (150m × 100m main hall)
**Features:**
- Vaulted ceiling (30m high)
- Marble floors (reflective)
- Information booth (central)
- Ticket counters
- Train platforms (below)
- Multiple exits/entrances
- Shops and restaurants lining hall
- Echoing acoustics

**NPCs:** High density (commuters, travelers, workers)
**Times:** Busiest 7-9 AM, 5-7 PM
**Interior:** Explorable (multiple levels, tunnels)
**Trains:** Operate on schedule (visual/audio spectacle)
**Reward:** Transportation hub, NPC dialogue opportunities, audio environment

#### **The Plaza** (Central Square)
**Location:** Intersection of Main Ave + Wall Street
**Scale:** Large civic square (200m × 200m)
**Features:**
- Central fountain (20m × 20m)
- Sitting areas (abundant benches)
- Retail shops surrounding
- Street performers/musicians
- Event stage (seasonal)
- Seasonal decorations
- Busy pedestrian flow

**NPCs:** Very high density (commuters, shoppers, tourists)
**Peak:** Lunch hours (12-1 PM), evenings (5-7 PM)
**Reward:** Social hub, NPC interactions, people-watching

#### **Sky Bridge Network** (Exploration)
**Location:** Spanning between towers (20+ bridges total)
**Scale:** Network of elevated enclosed walkways (2-3m wide)
**Features:**
- Elevated 15-40m above street
- Climate-controlled (glass enclosed)
- Shops/cafes within bridges
- Dramatic views
- Connecting multiple towers

**Access:** Building lobbies, elevator access
**Rewards:** Shortcut through district, elevated views, aerial perspective
**NPCs:** Moderate (office workers)
**Time:** Business hours primarily

#### **The Department Store** (Interior Exploration)
**Location:** Southern anchor of main avenue
**Scale:** 8-story interior (200m × 150m footprint)
**Features:**
- Grand atrium entry
- Escalators (visible, moving)
- Department floors (clothing, electronics, furniture)
- Central cafe (food court style)
- Rooftop parking access
- Multiple exit points
- Window displays seasonal themes

**Interior Exploration:** Can explore multiple floors
**NPCs:** Moderate (shoppers, employees)
**Times:** 9 AM - 9 PM
**Reward:** Interior visual variety, shopping ambiance, rooftop access

#### **The Subway System** (Underground Exploration)
**Location:** Beneath main streets
**Scale:** Network of tunnels, 3 major stations + 6 minor stops
**Features:**
- Tiled walls (utilitarian aesthetic)
- Platform areas (150m × 15m typical)
- Tracks with active trains
- Tunnel passages (long corridors)
- Signage and wayfinding
- Emergency lighting
- Abandoned sections (lore)

**Hazards:** Moving trains, electrical rails, water
**Access:** Stairs from Grand Central, other station entries
**Rewards:** Underground shortcuts, urban exploration, hidden passages
**Audio:** Train sounds, announcements, echoing ambiance

#### **Neon Alley** (Atmospheric)
**Location:** Side street off main avenue
**Scale:** 200m long narrow alley
**Features:**
- Dense neon signage (Japanese-style aesthetic)
- Narrow passage (5m wide)
- Restaurant storefronts
- Hanging signage
- Dramatic color contrasts
- Wet pavement reflections (always looks rainy)

**Best Viewing:** Night time (7 PM - midnight)
**NPCs:** Moderate (diners, workers)
**Ambiance:** Moody, mysterious, energetic
**Reward:** Unique visual environment, photo opportunity

### 2.5 Street Furniture & Details

**Lighting:**
- Modern streetlights (every 15m)
- High-powered (brighter than residential)

**Signs & Advertising:**
- Building signage (corporate logos)
- Storefronts (various design styles)
- Billboards (digital screens)
- Street art (murals on some walls)
- Directional signage

### 2.6 Time-of-Day Activities

**Early Morning (6-8 AM):**
- Light traffic
- Minimal pedestrians
- Delivery vehicles active
- Streetlights still on
- Shops opening

**Morning Rush (8-10 AM):**
- Heavy foot traffic
- Commuter crush
- Crowded crosswalks
- Fast-paced energy
- Coffee shops slammed

**Midday (10 AM-2 PM):**
- Peak shopping
- Lunch crowds

**Afternoon (2-5 PM):**

**Evening Rush (5-7 PM):**

**Night (7 PM-Midnight):**

## NEIGHBORHOOD 3: INDUSTRIAL DISTRICT (NE) — "The Yards"

### 3.1 Overall Character
- **Vibe:** Gritty, urban edge, artistic, abandoned
- **Population:** 2,000-5,000 (mostly workers/artists)
- **Time Peak:** 8 AM-5 PM (working hours)
- **Architecture Era:** Industrial (1920s-1980s) + Artist conversions
- **Primary Colors:** Rust red, concrete gray, blacks, graffiti art colors

### 3.2 Geography & Layout

**Block Structure:**
- Irregular grid (irregular spacing)
- Large building footprints (80-150m)
- Wide streets (14-18m) for truck traffic
- Minimal sidewalks in places (industrial)
- Elevated structures (cranes, loading docks)

**Elevation:**
- Varies ±30m (more dramatic)
- Steep ramps to loading docks
- River level areas (slight flooding risk)

**Key Streets:**
1. **Warehouse Row:** Main east-west corridor
   - Large rectangular warehouses
   - Heavy truck traffic (morning/afternoon)
   - Loading docks every 100m

2. **Factory Road:** North-south industrial spine
   - Manufacturing facilities
   - Smokestacks (visual landmarks)
   - Rail spur access

3. **Art Alley:** Secondary street (artist district)
   - Converted warehouse studios
   - Gallery spaces
   - Street art (murals, installations)
   - Pedestrian focus

### 3.3 Building Typology

#### Type A: Traditional Warehouse (50% of structures)
**Characteristics:**
- 3-5 stories (12-25m height)
- Rectangular footprint (100m × 40m typical)
- Exposed brick facades (red/brown)
- Large industrial windows
- Rooftop mechanisms (pulleys, vents)
- Minimal architectural details

**Features:**
- Large open interior spaces
- Multiple loading docks (1-2 per side)
- Exposed structure visible from outside
- Weathered aesthetic
- Water/weather damage visible

**Interior:** Explorable (industrial empty spaces)

#### Type B: Factory Complex (25% of structures)
**Characteristics:**
- Multi-section connected structures
- 4-8 stories in places
- Smokestacks/chimneys
- Massive scale (150m × 100m+ footprints)
- Heavy machinery visible through windows

**Features:**
- Assembly floor visible inside
- Rail spurs for logistics
- Rooftop obstacles (machinery)
- Multiple interconnected buildings
- Internal courts/alleys

#### Type C: Converted Lofts (20% of structures)
**Special:** Artist/startup occupancy
**Characteristics:**
- Former warehouses adapted
- Large window installations
- Ground-floor gallery/retail
- Apartments above
- Modern aesthetic clashing with industrial bones

**Interior:** Mix of residential and commercial

#### Type D: Specialty Buildings (5% of structures)
**Types:**
- Rail station (rail yard hub)
- Printing facility
- Auto repair shops
- Truck depot
- Power/utility stations

### 3.4 Points of Interest

#### **Abandoned Factory** (Major Exploration)
**Location:** Northeast corner of district
**Scale:** Massive complex (200m × 150m across 4 connected buildings)
**Features:**
- Crumbling brick walls
- Broken windows (glass pieces)
- Rusted machinery inside (visible)
- Multiple entry points
- Collapsed sections (unstable)
- Water accumulation (basement flooding)
- Stairs, ladders, catwalks (internal)
- Rooftop access possible

**Hazards:** Unstable floors, height, water, darkness
**NPCs:** Minimal (occasional artist/explorer)
**Reward:** Exploration destination, hidden items, urban archaeology
**Aesthetic:** Moody, mysterious, post-industrial

#### **Street Art Corridor** (Artistic Hub)
**Location:** Southern alley (Art Alley)
**Scale:** 400m long corridor (6-8m wide)
**Features:**
- Mural walls (both sides)
- Changing artwork (seasonal refresh)
- Gallery storefronts
- Vendor stalls (artist goods)
- Sitting areas
- Casual ambiance

**NPCs:** Moderate (artists, tourists, young creatives)
**Times:** 10 AM - 6 PM (artist hours)
**Reward:** Cultural hub, NPC interactions, visual interest
**Dynamic:** Murals change, reflecting seasonal/cultural themes

#### **Rail Yards** (Infrastructure)
**Location:** Western section of district
**Scale:** Large open area (400m × 300m)
**Features:**
- Multiple rail tracks
- Parked freight trains
- Switching mechanism
- Loading equipment
- Signal towers
- Sparse overhead lines

**Hazards:** Trains move (schedule-based), high voltage electricity
**Access:** Limited (fenced areas mostly)
**Reward:** Industrial spectacle, train watching, crossing challenges

#### **Container Port** (Parkour Zone)
**Location:** Southern district edge
**Scale:** Stacked shipping containers (20m high in places)
**Features:**
- Hundreds of containers (color-coded)
- Stacked in pyramids
- Forklift movement area (hazard)
- Cranes overhead
- Loading docks
- Network of accessible routes between stacks

**Rewards:** Parkour challenges, elevated perspectives, hidden routes
**Hazards:** Heavy equipment, falling containers (rare)
**Dynamic:** Containers move (cranes reposition)

#### **Artist Studios Gallery** (Social)
**Location:** Converted warehouse on Art Alley
**Scale:** Large open 3-story loft (80m × 40m)
**Features:**
- Ground floor: Public gallery space
- Upper floors: Artist studios (some visible)
- Installation art (rotating)
- Open-air courtyard
- Cafe corner
- Performance space

**NPCs:** Artists, visitors, patrons
**Times:** 11 AM - 7 PM (Thu-Sun primarily)
**Reward:** Cultural interaction, unique aesthetic, artist dialogue

#### **Graffiti Halls** (Underground)
**Location:** Utility tunnels beneath industrial district
**Scale:** Network of 2-3m high passages (500m+ total)
**Features:**
- Concrete walls (covered in art)
- Water channels (some flooded)
- Steam pipes overhead
- Emergency lighting
- Access grates (climbable)
- Urban art gallery aesthetic

**Hazards:** Water hazards, steam (hot), claustrophobic
**Rewards:** Underground network shortcut, visual spectacle
**NPCs:** Minimal (occasional artists)

### 3.5 Street Furniture & Details

**Lighting:**
- Industrial security lights (sparse)
- Harsh shadows
- Dark evening atmosphere
- Streetlights broken/missing (atmospheric)
- Accent lighting on murals (night)

**Vehicles:**
- Parked trucks (frequent)
- Active cargo vehicles (movement)
- Forklifts (movement, hazard)
- Rail locomotives (scheduled)
- Minimal traffic (unlike commercial)

**Pedestrian Features:**
- Minimal benches
- Informal seating areas
- Vendor stalls (mobile)
- Storage containers (functional)
- No formal landscaping

**Greenery:**
- Minimal (urban agriculture gardens emerging)
- Ivy on abandoned walls
- Weeds breaking through
- Occasional potted plants (artistic)

**Signs & Art:**
- Stencil art everywhere
- Mural art (professional quality)
- Graffiti tags (urban aesthetic)
- Faded signage (historical)
- Industrial warning signs
- Building number markers

### 3.6 Time-of-Day Activities

**Early Morning (6-8 AM):**
- Workers arriving
- Truck traffic building
- Industrial sounds increasing
- Minimal pedestrians
- Cool light

**Working Hours (8 AM-5 PM):**
- Heavy truck/forklift activity
- Industrial sounds (machinery, horns)
- Workers visible
- Loading/unloading activity
- Dust/grime visible in light

**After Hours (5-8 PM):**
- Activity drops sharply
- Artists emerge
- Evening light on murals (golden)
- Quieter ambiance
- Fewer vehicles

**Night (8 PM-6 AM):**
- Mostly empty
- Security lights primary
- Occasional night workers
- Eerie, echo-y
- Artist activities (graffiti refresh)

### 3.7 Vertical Exploration Routes

**Factory Rooftops:**
- Accessible via internal stairs/ladders
- Connected via bridges (some)
- Machinery to navigate around
- Height hazards
- Panoramic views

**Container Climbing:**
- Stack navigable via container edges
- Exposed to weather
- Height progression challenge
- Rewarding views

**Underground Network:**
- Utility tunnels accessible from gratings
- Artistic passages
- Water hazard areas
- Alternative routes through district

## NEIGHBORHOOD 4: PARK/NATURE DISTRICT (SW) — "Crystal Valley"

### 4.1 Overall Character
- **Vibe:** Peaceful, natural, explorative, serene
- **Population:** 1,000-3,000 (visitors, not residents)
- **Time Peak:** Morning (7-9 AM), Evening (5-7 PM)
- **Architecture Era:** Natural + Historical structures
- **Primary Colors:** Greens, blues, browns, natural stone grays

### 4.2 Geography & Layout

**Terrain:**
- Rolling hills (±40m elevation variation)
- Multiple valleys with watercourses
- Open meadows (grassland)
- Dense forest sections
- Rock outcrops (climbing features)
- Natural lake (1km × 0.8km)

**Water Features:**
- Crystal Lake (central)
- River flowing through (tributary)
- Multiple ponds (smaller)
- Waterfalls (2-3)
- Springs (fresh water)
- Streams (flowing through forests)

**Path Network:**
- Main looped paths (wide, maintained)
- Secondary trails (narrower)
- Hidden paths (shortcuts, shortcuts)
- Natural obstacles (logs, rocks)

### 4.3 Key Locations

#### **Crystal Lake** (Centerpiece)
**Scale:** Large water body (1km × 0.8km, 20-40m deep)
**Features:**
- Mirror-like surface (clear days)
- Sandy beach (southwest corner)
- Rocky shoreline (northeast)
- Tree-lined shores
- Small dock area
- Boat rentals (NPC interaction)
- Island in center (accessible by boat)

**Activities:**
- Swimming (shallow areas)
- Boating exploration
- Fishing (NPC activity)
- Scenic viewing
- Reflection/meditation

**NPCs:** Moderate (swimmers, fishermen, visitors, ducks)
**Best Time:** Midday (clear weather), sunset (golden hour)
**Reward:** Central hub location, water exploration, scenic beauty

#### **Enchanted Forest** (Exploration)
**Location:** Northern section of park
**Scale:** Dense forest (800m × 600m)
**Features:**
