================================================================================
CAT WALK: RECOMMENDED PROGRESSION FLOW
================================================================================
Version: 1.0
Date: 2026-07-10
Strategy: Hybrid (Story-driven narrative with Discovery mechanics + Challenge endgame)
Target: 60+ hours for full completion, 20-30 hours for main story

================================================================================
PHASE 1: ONBOARDING & TUTORIAL (Hours 0-2, Levels 1-2)
================================================================================

STORY FRAME: "Waking Up"
The player wakes up confused in Downtown, disoriented. Luna (first NPC) greets
the player warmly and explains the situation: "You've wandered away from home.
Let me help you find your way."

LOCATION: Downtown (Central Commercial District)
ACCESS: All Downtown locations unlocked from start

PLAYER PROGRESSION:
└─ Progress: 0% → 10%

MECHANICS INTRODUCED (One per 15 minutes):
  ✓ Movement (walk/run) - First 5 minutes
  ✓ Jumping - Minutes 5-10
  ✓ Camera/Look controls - Minutes 5-15
  ✓ Knockable objects - Minutes 15-20
  ✓ Basic climbing (trees) - Minutes 20-25
  ✓ NPC interaction (meowing) - Minutes 20-30
  ✓ UI/HUD explanation - Throughout

QUESTS:
  1. STORY: "Find Home" - Explore Downtown, meet Luna
     └─ Reward: +100 XP, Downtown 25% explored
  2. ACTIVITY: "Knock 10 Objects" - Practice with light knockables
     └─ Reward: +50 XP, Satisfaction +10%
  3. ACTIVITY: "Climb First Tree" - Reach 5m height
     └─ Reward: +75 XP, Confidence +5%

NPC INTERACTIONS:
  └─ Luna: Friendly greeting, explains situation, offers guidance

SKILLS UNLOCKED:
  ├─ Walking (default)
  ├─ Running (tutorial teaches at 1:30)
  ├─ Jumping (tutorial teaches at 5:00)
  └─ Basic Knock (automatic on first object hit)

COLLECTIBLES DISCOVERED:
  ├─ Yarn Ball (red, downtown plaza) - Teaching item
  └─ Tutorial Item: Fish Treat (free item from Luna)

COMPLETION CONDITION: Reach "Your Home" location in residential zone
  └─ Cutscene: Luna shows you to a cozy spot, explains she lives nearby

DIFFICULTY SCALING:
  └─ All challenges tuned to teach, not challenge
  └─ Auto-aim assists enabled by default
  └─ Generous stamina pool for tutorial

END CONDITION: Player wakes up in home location, ready to explore


================================================================================
PHASE 2: EXPLORATION & DISCOVERY (Hours 2-10, Levels 3-7)
================================================================================

STORY FRAME: "Getting Oriented"
Player explores their new neighborhood, making friends, learning the lay of
the land. Luna suggests exploring nearby areas and introduces the concept of
building relationships.

LOCATION: Residential + Parks (open world, ~1 km²)
ACCESS: Downtown always accessible. Parks unlock at 15-minute mark.
GATING: Soft gates - NPCs suggest areas but don't prevent exploration

PLAYER PROGRESSION:
└─ Progress: 10% → 35%

PACING: 45 minutes per major location (3 locations × 2 hours)

PHASE 2A: RESIDENTIAL ZONE (Hours 2-4)
─────────────────────────────────────
First neighborhood exploration. Friendly zone with moderate challenges.

QUESTS (Choose 3 of 5):
  1. "Meet the Neighborhood" - Visit 5 houses, meow at each
     └─ Reward: +150 XP, +1 relationship to each NPC
  2. "Garden Exploration" - Collect 10 items from gardens
     └─ Reward: +100 XP, inventory expansion +5
  3. "Roof Tour" - Climb 3 houses and reach roofs
     └─ Reward: +125 XP, climbing confidence +10%
  4. "Knockable Training" - Knock over 20 objects
     └─ Reward: +75 XP, satisfaction +15%
  5. "First Friendship" - Build +20 relationship with any cat
     └─ Reward: +100 XP, first friend badge

NPC ENCOUNTERS:
  ├─ Luna (again, in garden) - Relationship building
  ├─ Whiskers (curious young cat) - Friendly, wants to play
  ├─ Mittens (family cat, shy) - Neutral, warms up quickly
  └─ Scarecrow NPC (human) - Shoos you away but not aggressively

SKILLS UNLOCKED (Optional - auto-trigger on use):
  ├─ Sprint Boost: Hold run button longer (stamina cost: 5/sec)
  ├─ Climbing Basics: Can climb wooden fences
  └─ Friendly Meow: NPCs get +5 starting relationship

COLLECTIBLES:
  ├─ Yarn Ball (red, blue) - 5 total in residential
  ├─ Fish Treat - 3 hidden spots
  └─ Feather - 2 floating in wind

CHALLENGES (Optional):
  └─ "Race to the Mailbox" - 60-second navigation challenge
     └─ Reward: +50 XP, time tracked for speedrun

DIFFICULTY RAMPING:
  ├─ Jumps get slightly higher requirement
  ├─ Some climbs require 2+ attempts
  └─ NPCs mention harder areas exist (psychological prep)


PHASE 2B: PARKS ZONE (Hours 4-7)
─────────────────────────────────
Larger open area with more collectibles and NPCs. Introduction to
intermediate challenges.

UNLOCK TRIGGER: Complete 1 residential quest OR explore for 30 minutes
  └─ Luna mentions: "There's a beautiful park not far from here!"

QUESTS (Choose 4 of 6):
  1. "Park Exploration" - Visit all 4 park locations
     └─ Reward: +200 XP, area 40% unlocked
  2. "Bird Watching" - Spot 10 birds, feather collectible reward
     └─ Reward: +100 XP, feathers x3
  3. "Friends Gathering" - Build +30 relationship with 2 NPCs
     └─ Reward: +150 XP, social skill +5%
  4. "Obstacle Course" - Complete 3-jump parkour sequence
     └─ Reward: +125 XP, parkour confidence +15%
  5. "Chaos Unleashed" - Knock 30 objects in the park
     └─ Reward: +100 XP, satisfaction +25%
  6. "Hidden Cache" - Find secret spot with 5 collectibles
     └─ Reward: +175 XP, treasure finder badge

NPC ENCOUNTERS:
  ├─ Luna (frequent mentor) - Always friendly, gives hints
  ├─ Whiskers (new friendship) - Play invitations
  ├─ Tiger (active, athletic cat) - Suggests challenges
  ├─ Small Children (human NPCs) - Want to play, generous gifts
  └─ Park Vendor (human) - Offers small trades

SKILLS UNLOCKED (On use):
  ├─ Power Knock: Heavier knockables move farther
  ├─ Persuasion: NPC relationship gains +25%
  └─ Stamina Recovery: Rest regenerates stamina 50% faster

COLLECTIBLES:
  ├─ Yarn Ball (yellow, purple, green) - 8 total
  ├─ Fish Treat - 5 hidden
  ├─ Feather - 10 scattered
  └─ Secret: Golden Yarn Ball x1 (legendary collectible)

CHALLENGES:
  ├─ "Park Parkour" - 5-jump sequence under 45 seconds
  ├─ "Shadow Chase" - Catch Tiger in playtime game
  └─ "Knockdown Championship" - Speedrun knocking objects

DIFFICULTY RAMPING:
  ├─ Double-jump hints emerge (not yet unlocked)
  ├─ Some areas require sprint to reach
  ├─ First time seeing territorial cat (foreshadowing)
  └─ Weather can affect gameplay (rain = slippery surfaces)


PHASE 2C: INDUSTRIAL PREVIEW (Hours 7-10)
──────────────────────────────────────────
Optional early exploration of harder area. No quests, just discovery.
Sets up curiosity for Act 2.

UNLOCK TRIGGER: Complete 4+ park/residential quests OR 2+ hours of exploration
  └─ Shadow (territorial cat) mentions: "Beyond the market is another world..."

QUESTS: NONE (Free exploration)
OPTIONAL: Navigation challenges, collectible hunting

NPC ENCOUNTERS:
  ├─ Shadow (first territorial cat) - Cool, not hostile
  ├─ Sage (mysterious elder cat) - Very aloof, barely acknowledges you
  └─ Workers (human NPCs) - Busy, ignore you mostly

VIBE: Hints at danger, mystery, growth
EXPLORATION REWARD: Finding items here gives 2x XP (rare location bonus)

PROGRESSION GATE: Encourage return later with better skills
  └─ Some areas blocked (tall fences, tough climbs) = "You need more practice"


PHASE 2 END CONDITION:
─────────────────────
- Complete 5+ quests from Residential/Parks
- Build +20 relationship with at least 2 NPCs
- Discover 30+ collectible items
- Reach Level 7 (XP threshold)
- Unlock at least 1 skill beyond starting mechanics

PHASE 2 OUTCOME:
├─ Player comfort with: Movement, jumping, basic climbing, NPC interaction
├─ Player discovery of: Knockables, relationship system, skill unlocks
├─ Player curiosity about: Industrial zone, mysterious NPCs, story hooks
└─ Player stats: 35% exploration, 5+ relationships, 30+ collectibles


================================================================================
PHASE 3: ADVENTURE & CHALLENGE (Hours 10-25, Levels 8-14)
================================================================================

STORY FRAME: "The Mystery Deepens"
Player builds on early relationships and discovers a larger mystery. Industrial
zone opens up. The concept of "The Gateway" is introduced through NPC dialogue.
Player becomes more skilled and attempts harder challenges.

LOCATION: Industrial Zone + Waterfront + Return to previous areas
ACCESS: Industrial and Waterfront unlock. All previous zones accessible.
GATING: Story quests unlock industrial areas. Waterfront accessible after
        building relationship with specific NPCs.

PLAYER PROGRESSION:
└─ Progress: 35% → 70%

PHASE 3A: INDUSTRIAL ZONE (Hours 10-16)
────────────────────────────────────────
Dangerous, complex area. First real challenge zone. Introduces combat/parkour.

UNLOCK: Complete "Industrial Rumor" quest from Shadow or Sage
STORY: Sage hints that industrial zone holds secrets about The Gateway

QUESTS (Primary Story - Must Complete):
  1. "Industrial Secrets" - Gather 3 artifact tokens from industrial zone
     └─ Reward: +300 XP, major story progression
  2. "Warehouse Raid" - Navigate complex warehouse, parkour section
     └─ Reward: +250 XP, unlock climbing skill boost
  3. "Shadow's Challenge" - Defeat Shadow in competitive game (playtime)
     └─ Reward: +200 XP, +50 relationship with Shadow, unlock zone

QUESTS (Optional):
  4. "Destruction Derby" - Knock down 50 objects in industrial yard
     └─ Reward: +150 XP, satisfaction +50%
  5. "Height Challenge" - Climb tallest building in industrial zone
     └─ Reward: +175 XP, climbing confidence +25%
  6. "Parkour Gauntlet" - Complete 10-section parkour course under 3 minutes
     └─ Reward: +200 XP, parkour skill unlock

NPC ENCOUNTERS:
  ├─ Shadow (again, mentor figure) - Becomes ally, teaches skills
  ├─ Sage (mysterious elder) - Reveals partial story about prophecy
  ├─ Dock Workers (human) - Neutral, talk about supplies
  ├─ Factory Manager (human) - Blocks access to some areas
  └─ Scarlet (dangerous cat) - First real antagonist, territorial

SKILLS UNLOCKED (Major):
  ├─ Agility I: +0.5m jump height, +20% climbing speed
  ├─ Wall Run: Slide down walls, parkour essential skill
  ├─ Power Knock Advanced: Break ceramic objects
  ├─ Stealth: Crouch and move quietly
  └─ Locator Sense: Highlights nearby collectibles

COLLECTIBLES:
  ├─ Story Artifacts (3 total) - Required for progression
  ├─ Yarn Ball (8 different colors) - 10+ scattered
  ├─ Feather - 15 hidden
  ├─ Special: Factory Parts - Craft-able items
  └─ Rare: Ancient Coins - Trade for special items

CHALLENGES:
  ├─ "Industrial Speedrun" - Navigate zone in <8 minutes
  ├─ "Parkour Master Trial" - Complete complex wall-run sequence
  └─ "Destruction Challenge" - Cause >$50,000 in virtual damage

DIFFICULTY:
  ├─ First NPCs that can defeat player
  ├─ Falls are actually dangerous (damage system introduced)
  ├─ Time pressure on some challenges
  ├─ Climbs require actual skill (not just button mashing)
  └─ Multiple paths to achieve goals (player agency)

WORLD EVENTS:
  ├─ Day 1: Quiet, exploratory
  ├─ Day 2: Workers arrive, area busier, harder to navigate
  ├─ Day 3: Storm warning (weather mechanic introduced)
  └─ Day 4: Complete industrial section, unlock next area


PHASE 3B: WATERFRONT ZONE (Hours 16-21)
────────────────────────────────────────
Beautiful, mysterious water area. Introduces water mechanics and fishing.
Continues story of The Gateway.

UNLOCK: Complete industrial main quest OR build +40 relationship with any NPC
STORY: Sage reveals that Gateway is "at the convergence of land and water"

QUESTS (Primary Story):
  1. "Harbor Secrets" - Explore waterfront, find 2 story artifacts
     └─ Reward: +300 XP, major story progression
  2. "Fishing Expedition" - Catch 5 fish using new mechanics
     └─ Reward: +200 XP, unlock new food source
  3. "Sage's Wisdom" - Build +50 relationship with Sage, unlock dialogue
     └─ Reward: +250 XP, story revelation

QUESTS (Optional):
  4. "Boat Exploration" - Climb boats, find hidden spots
     └─ Reward: +150 XP, navigation unlock
  5. "Water's Edge" - Master swimming/water mechanics
     └─ Reward: +175 XP, water confidence +30%
  6. "Lighthouse Climb" - Reach top of lighthouse (highest point yet)
     └─ Reward: +200 XP, victory view unlock

NPC ENCOUNTERS:
  ├─ Sage (mentor figure, reveals more) - Major story dialogue
  ├─ Fisherman (human) - Teaches fishing, trades supplies
  ├─ Marina Workers (humans) - Various trades
  ├─ Seagulls (animal NPCs) - Antagonistic, comedic
  └─ Dolphin (rare encounter) - Secret, magical moment

SKILLS UNLOCKED:
  ├─ Swimming: Navigate water at 2 m/s
  ├─ Fishing: Catch fish using timing mini-game
  ├─ Diving: Hold breath, explore underwater (limited)
  ├─ Water Resistance: Rain/wet doesn't slow you down
  └─ Boat Mastery: Climb and traverse boats safely

COLLECTIBLES:
  ├─ Fish (5 types, can eat)
  ├─ Shells (decorative, tradeable)
  ├─ Story Artifacts (2 total)
  ├─ Pearls (rare, legendary)
  └─ Fishing Trophies: Catch records

CHALLENGES:
  ├─ "Fishing Competition" - Catch most fish in 10 minutes
  ├─ "Lighthouse Speed Run" - Climb to top in <4 minutes
  └─ "Ocean Explorer" - Visit 15 water locations

DIFFICULTY:
  ├─ Water mechanics introduce new challenge
  ├─ Fishing is skill-based mini-game
  ├─ Some locations require specific skills
  └─ Story revelations build stakes

PACING: Slower, more contemplative than industrial zone


PHASE 3C: SUBURBS UNLOCK (Hours 21-25)
───────────────────────────────────────
Peaceful residential area accessed after industrial + waterfront.
More exploration, fewer combat challenges.

UNLOCK: Complete industrial + waterfront main quests
STORY: "You're getting closer to understanding The Gateway..."

QUESTS (Optional, no main story):
  1. "Suburb Exploration" - Visit all 6 suburban neighborhoods
  2. "Quiet Life" - Build relationships with 5 new NPCs
  3. "Perfect Homes" - Explore 10 houses completely

NPC ENCOUNTERS:
  ├─ Domestic cats - More friendly, less territorial
  ├─ Families - Humans with children
  └─ Retirees - Wise elders with stories

VIBE: Peaceful, warm, reflective
PROGRESSION: Not required for story, but essential for 100% completion

PHASE 3 END CONDITION:
──────────────────────
- Complete main quests: Industrial + Waterfront
- Build +50 relationship with Sage and Shadow
- Discover 4+ story artifacts
- Reach Level 14 (XP threshold)
- Unlock 5+ advanced skills

PHASE 3 OUTCOME:
├─ Player understanding: Larger story, world depth, advanced mechanics
├─ Player discovery: The Gateway mystery, NPC backstories
├─ Player ability: Advanced parkour, combat skills, water mastery
├─ Player stats: 70% exploration, 10+ relationships, 100+ collectibles

PHASE 4: CLIMAX & REVELATION (Hours 25-35, Levels 15-18)
================================================================================

STORY FRAME: "The Gateway Revealed"
The mystery comes to a head. Player discovers The Gateway location. Major
story revelations. Preparation for final challenge.

LOCATION: Secret Areas + Return to key locations
ACCESS: Unlocks hidden areas not accessible before
GATING: Story gates enforce order of discovery

PLAYER PROGRESSION:
└─ Progress: 70% → 90%

PHASE 4A: SECRET AREAS DISCOVERED (Hours 25-30)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────
Hidden locations that exist in parallel to main world.

UNLOCK: Build +75 relationship with Sage
TRIGGER: Sage reveals secret location in cryptic dialogue

QUESTS:
  1. "Hidden Garden" - Explore secret garden area, collect rare items
  2. "Underground Tunnels" - Navigate mysterious tunnel system
  3. "The Convergence" - Find The Gateway location
  4. "Final Ritual" - Gather 5 sacred tokens from across world

STORY BEATS:
  ├─ Sage explains ancient prophecy about The Gateway
  ├─ Revelation: Player is descendant of legendary cat explorer
  ├─ Discovery: The Gateway is not physical, it's spiritual
  └─ Challenge: Must prove worthiness through trials

SKILLS UNLOCKED (Master Tier):
  ├─ Agility II: Double-jump, +50% climb speed
  ├─ Predator Vision: See hidden paths and secrets
  ├─ Master Meow: Cats from across city come to your aid
  └─ Legendary Presence: All NPCs start at +50 relationship in new games

COLLECTIBLES:
  ├─ Ancient Artifacts (5, crucial for story)
  ├─ Legendary Items (hidden, legendary tier)
  └─ Easter Eggs (secret references, unlocks cosmetics)

DIFFICULTY:
  ├─ Puzzles introduce element-based challenges
  ├─ Some require creative use of mechanics
  ├─ NPCs test your skills through competitive games
  └─ Emotional stakes increase

ZONE FEELS:
  ├─ Hidden Garden: Peaceful, beautiful, magical
  ├─ Tunnels: Dark, mysterious, slightly scary
  ├─ The Convergence: Epic, grand, awe-inspiring


PHASE 4B: THE GATEWAY TRIAL (Hours 30-35)
──────────────────────────────────────────
Final challenge before endgame. Culmination of all learned skills.

UNLOCK: Find The Gateway location + gather all 5 sacred tokens

MAIN QUEST: "Gateway Trial"
├─ Stage 1: Prove Agility (parkour gauntlet, 3 minutes)
├─ Stage 2: Prove Wisdom (solve 3 environmental puzzles)
├─ Stage 3: Prove Kindness (complete NPC tasks without failing)
├─ Stage 4: Prove Courage (face fears, survive challenges)
└─ Final: Face The Guardian (boss encounter, skill check)

BOSS ENCOUNTER: The Guardian
├─ Not a combat boss, more a skill demonstration
├─ Requires creative use of all learned mechanics
├─ Multiple solutions, player agency in approach
├─ Defeat triggers ending sequence

STORY CLIMAX:
├─ Reveal nature of The Gateway
├─ Player becomes Legend status
├─ All NPCs acknowledge your achievement
├─ Unlock epilogue content

REWARDS:
├─ +500 XP (largest reward in game)
├─ Legendary Badge
├─ New Game+ unlock
├─ All cosmetics unlock
└─ Credits roll, epilogue begins

PHASE 4 END CONDITION:
──────────────────────
- Complete Gateway Trial
- Reach Legend status
- Defeat The Guardian
- Reach Level 18

PHASE 4 OUTCOME:
├─ Story complete
├─ Main world 90% explored
├─ All NPCs at Legendary relationship
├─ Player satisfied with narrative arc
