04

Back to the valley

What would move into the game, and what it would cost.

  • Rocks are cheap now. A chiselled icosphere is about 20k triangles at detail 5, which is too much to repeat. Detail 3 keeps the silhouette and halves it twice over; use the higher detail only on hero stones.
  • Share the shapes. Three or four rock meshes instanced with random scale and yaw cover the valley. Moss comes from the world-space normal, so rotation does not spoil it.
  • Keep the key rock’s collider. buildKeyHidingRock() has a collider and a hiding spot behind it. A new mesh just needs the same footprint.
  • Gravel raking is a canvas. The rake tool draws into the plot’s 2k canvas. The game’s sandō could bake a raked pattern the same way at load.
