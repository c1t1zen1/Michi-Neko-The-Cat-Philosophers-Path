04

Back to the valley

What would move into the game, and what it would cost.

  • Move the logic. Let the water drive it. A small fill number replaces the timer in main.js, and the rhythm then varies with the weather if you want it to.
  • Keep what the rocker does. Michi already reacts to it and the tea house quest depends on it, so it needs the same name and the same attach point.
  • Sound budget is small. All of this is synthesised, with no samples. Each sound is a few oscillators or a short noise burst.
  • The jar is a place worth hiding. Suikinkutsu would suit the Moon Pool or the tea house garden: a place you hear before you find it.
