Back to the valley

What would move into the game, and what it would cost.

  • One culm mesh. The fence becomes a single InstancedMesh of culms plus one of knots. The game’s fence today is about twenty meshes for a short run.
  • Keep what the corral does. The corral’s reward, sign and Larry the turtle are gameplay. The fences can be replaced, the rest stays.
  • Colliders are simple. Each drawn segment already acts as a thin capsule for the cat, and that maps onto the game’s continuous fence collider.
  • Age is a colour ramp. No new textures. One four-stop ramp over instance colours covers fresh cut to almost black.
