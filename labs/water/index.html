Water that knows
which way it flows

The river is the biggest mirror in the valley, and today it is a ribbon of sine waves that ignore its bends. This lab rebuilds it from the bed up: a flow field you can paint, real depth, foam where the current hits stone, reflections, and a painterly finish that sits between a photograph and a Ghibli background. Then the bridge, post by post and giboshi by giboshi, and the paddies through a year. Michi can walk all of it, hop the turtle stones, and jump off the bridge.
