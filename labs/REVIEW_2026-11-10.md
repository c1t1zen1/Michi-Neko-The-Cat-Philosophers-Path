# Labs construction review — 2026-11-10

Reviewed the 22 registered labs and Grass in source and browser captures.
Rendered **381 shelf specimens**, the 23 hero stages, and **nine Range Lab
bench views**. The review follows supports, joints, roof fall lines, openings,
water paths, material assignments and animated attachment points.

## What changed

* **Torii:** solve the lintel's underside at each leaning post, rather than
  setting the lintel at an unrelated height. The kasagi and shimaki share
  one curve. Miwa side openings share their inner supports with the main gate.
* **Shrine buildings:** closed roof skins and gables, grounded steps, a gable
  entrance and white infill for Kasuga, and supported extended eaves for Nagare.
  Stair approaches clear the railings, and the Kasuga porch bears on square posts and a beam.
  Hiwada is identified as cypress bark. The chōzuya has bearing beams, a hollow
  trough, recessed water, supported supply, a dipper rack and a hollow ladle.
* **Water garden:** a grounded riser, lashed trestle and straight hollow feeder;
  a hollow diagonal receiver with a closed bamboo node; a physical axle and
  a tail that meets its strike stone. The pour follows the moving mouth.
  The basin is carved out, and the water-harp jar has its top hole and cutaway.
  The fūrin opens downwards and hangs from a suspension cord.
* **Lanterns:** real apertures into a hollow stone firebox replace the glowing
  belt. Both kotoji legs meet the table; its short leg lands on a stone.
* **Fences and plants:** a falling Kōetsu crown with clipped diagonal lattice;
  bamboo leaves attached to node-rooted twigs; explicit green tint on the
  previously white bush specimens.
* **Lane props:** hollow barrel interiors and ring-shaped hoops, slatted crates
  with corner battens, soil inside pots, and wall-anchored drying lines. Modern
  bicycles and shop tanuki remain selectable, outside the initial period palette.
* **Animals:** paired butterfly wings, correctly oriented koi with dorsal and
  forked tail fins, head markings kept off the fins, and initialized/mirrored
  flock instances so their camera fit and wing pair are correct.
* **Other supports:** corrected the Weather Lab's inverted V roof, the Ground
  Lab's outward-rising upper eave, the Nest Lab's unsupported roof section,
  and the Interiors Lab's short floating basin rack. The Villager Lab's lit
  panel has a timber shōji frame.
* **Shelf rendering:** flat water studies sit above their plinth instead of
  z-fighting with it. Depthless points, lines and sprites bypass the painted
  background replacement that erased them. Komorebi projections respect the
  specimen's model matrix and fit within their demonstration slab.

The labs are design studies of several dates. Current photographs establish
visible construction, not proof that every present-day detail existed in Edo.
Modern show koi, contemporary lamp experiments, chabudai interiors and fictional
collectibles remain labelled studies. Proportions and the water/sound models are
illustrative. No third-party reference photograph is bundled into the game or
republished in the gallery.

## Iteration and verification

1. Captured all stages and shelves before edits, against baseline commit
   `ec55cc7`. Read object builders and shared geometry/material helpers.
2. Researched and inspected the references above. Rebuilt disconnected or
   implausible assemblies and rendered them again.
3. The next close-up pass exposed insufficient sōzu travel, a koi UV reversal,
   supports inside the basin footprint, white shrub tint and erased shelf
   effects. Corrected these and recaptured. The final sōzu completes three
   clacks in a 16.7-second fixed-step run, ranging from −0.65 to +0.62 radians.
4. Preserved 12 fixed-camera object pairs plus five stage pairs in the gallery.
   Object pairs share camera/light; stage pairs explicitly note their changed
   framing. Nest uses the existing `built=1` state; Weather's poster uses `demo=1`.
5. Ran `node tools/labs_check.mjs`, `node tools/verify.mjs` and the browser
   [geometry suite](../tools/labs_geometry_test.html): **32/32 passed**. Raycasts
   verify pillar/lintel bearings and open firebox apertures; bounds and cycle
   checks cover the other repaired assemblies.
6. Browser captures used Chrome for Testing 153 with ANGLE Metal on the Mac.
   The 23-page sweep, 381 shelf cards and nine Range bench views produced no
   page exceptions, console errors or shader failures. This is not a phone or
   low-end GPU benchmark, and sound was not evaluated by listening.
7. Checked the portal and gallery at 1200 px and 390 px widths: no horizontal
   overflow or console errors. All 45 portal image elements and 34 gallery
   image elements returned HTTP 200.

The game modules, summit source/UI, companion apps and seeded valley layout
were not edited. Runtime artwork remains procedural; the added PNGs are portal
and review images.
