The ground under the paws

Grass covers most of the valley now, which leaves the bare ground as the flattest thing on screen: paths, clearings and the earth under the eaves. This lab rebuilds it. Paint earth, gravel, moss and stone, watch the layers settle into each other by height, make it rain, and follow Michi’s paw prints through the mud. Everything is painted at load time.

How this goes into the valley

  • The splat is the grass biome mask read the other way round: where the mask says no grass (paths, the shrine precinct, under the eaves), the ground shows earth, ishidatami or gravel.
  • buildPathShoulders() becomes a band in the splat instead of an alpha strip, so the path edge blends by height.
  • buildWalkwaySlabs() becomes ishidatami setts, with real stones near the cat.
  • Puddles follow the weather in main.js, and the trail buffer takes the same paw positions the grass physics already reads.
  • Amaochi strips go under every eave that has a collider, the same way buildings already get a grass-free footprint.
