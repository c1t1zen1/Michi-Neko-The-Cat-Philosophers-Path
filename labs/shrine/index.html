04

Back to the valley

What would move into the game, and what it would cost.

  • One curve, shared. buildTorii() can take a sori array and sweep the kasagi and shimaki along it. That replaces the four rotated boxes and removes the visible joints.
  • Keep the climbing route. The late-game pegs and the kasagi platforms are gameplay. A new gate has to keep them, at the same heights, or the City Legend route breaks.
  • Three draws a gate. Merged per material, a full-detail gate is three or four draws, and the slim senbon gate instances, so a long path of them is one draw per material.
  • New pieces need colliders. Komainu, the chōzuya and the paving are additions. Each needs a footprint in vegetationExclusions, and new materials need to live in MAT or _mergeStatics() will not merge them.
