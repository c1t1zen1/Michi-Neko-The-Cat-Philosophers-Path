04

Back to the valley

What would move into the game, and what it would cost.

  • Keep the shader bend. The game’s stalks are bent in the vertex shader for free. Swap the geometry and leave that alone.
  • Swap the cylinder for the node culm. One lathe with node bulges, instanced. It’s about four times the triangles of the current six-sided cylinder, so keep it to the nearest culms.
  • Growth is a one-off, or a season. The curve lets a replanted grove rise once at load, or follow the game’s calendar if it ever gets one.
  • Replace the street bushes last. The foliage shader is already good. Clipped karikomi is the addition, not a replacement.
