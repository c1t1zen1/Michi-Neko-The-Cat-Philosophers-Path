04

Back to the valley

What would move into the game, and what it would cost.

  • Keep the leaf sprays. The hero tier is the game's own look: individual sprays rooted on twigs, bending with the flex wind. Nothing here replaces it.
  • Fix the trunk. The classic trunk is a cylinder rotated about its middle, so its foot lifts off the ground when it leans. A spine that bends from a planted foot, with a root collar and buttress lobes, costs about 300 triangles.
  • Let the tier choose the canopy. Hero near the cat, thinner sprays at mid, painted masses at far: the same skeleton, a quarter of the draw cost.
  • One material per kind. The kit shares one foliage material per species instead of one per tree, so a future bake can merge them.
