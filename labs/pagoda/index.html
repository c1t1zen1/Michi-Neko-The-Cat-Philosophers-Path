Michi-Neko · Pagoda Lab

Seven ways to
rise into the sky

The distant pagoda (五重塔) is the landmark of Michi's valley — seen from the meadow, the bridge, and the meditation perch against the Kyoto basin. Here we pull it apart, compare seven procedural approaches to building it in Three.js, and test each against the research: the correct proportions, the full sōrin finial, the kumimono brackets, and a Ghibli-style painterly render that reads against the sky at any distance.

Research & sources

Nine sources informed these builders: structural engineering studies of the Hōryū-ji pagoda, architectural morphology analysis of Japanese timber towers, and detailed sōrin documentation.

  • IITK (WCEE) — Earthquake response of ancient five-story pagoda of Hōryū-ji (proportions, shinbashira)
  • JAABE — Structural mechanism and morphology of timber towers in Japan (kumimono system)
  • Rachel Stull — Sōrin (finial parts)
  • Discover Kyoto — Tō-ji (dimensions)
  • World History Encyclopedia — Kyoto's five-story pagoda at Tō-ji
  • NJCU — Structural characters of five-story pagoda
