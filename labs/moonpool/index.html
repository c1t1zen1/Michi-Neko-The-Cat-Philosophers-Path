04

Back to the valley

What would move into the game, and what it would cost.

  • Swap the disc, keep the place. The pool stays 3 m across behind the shrine. The new water replaces MeshStandardMaterial, and its CPU heightfield at 128² costs well under a millisecond.
  • Reflection is the expensive part. A second scene render is real money in the valley. Cap it to a 512 px target, draw only what sits near the pool, and skip it unless the pool is on screen.
  • The moon wants one owner. The sky’s sprite would need a phase and to appear in the reflection pass. The stage hides it and draws its own to show how that would work.
  • Keep the staggered kindling. The glade’s golden-ratio onset already looks right. Swap in the washi body and leave the schedule alone.
