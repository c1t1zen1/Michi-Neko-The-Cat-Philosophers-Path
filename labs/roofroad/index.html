    03

    Into the kit

    How a piece here becomes a production asset.

    • One id per piece type. rooftops/js/pieces/index.js maps every type to an inventory id and an owner lane. buildWorld() builds each piece through assets.build(id, layout, classic), so the classic builder runs until a kit asset registers that id.
    • The geometry is gameplay. Platforms, walls and ceilings come from level.js, never from a mesh. A kit piece draws the surfaces its level record names, at those heights.
    • Built in the rail frame. A kit piece is placed where the classic one stands: local x along the rail, z toward the camera, ground at y = 0. Sockets named light… become lantern lights.
    • Classic stays the default. The run page builds its own asset runtime: ?assets=modular or ?swap=roof-bridge:modular switch pieces; with no Roof Road kit asset, every piece is classic.
