04

Back to the valley

What would move into the game, and what it would cost.

  • Fold it into the ground. The whole effect is one instanced draw of about two thousand quads, additive, on the ground plane. It belongs inside the Ground Lab’s ground pass.
  • Shade first, then coins. The coins only look right in shade, so under-canopy ground needs its direct sun cut back, which the grass and ground already know how to do.
  • Scale from the trees. Each gap inherits its height from the canopy above. Gaps could come from the real tree positions instead of being scattered.
  • Bring the petals back. The game turned petal drifts off because the flat circles looked wrong. Instanced petals that lie down are a different thing.
