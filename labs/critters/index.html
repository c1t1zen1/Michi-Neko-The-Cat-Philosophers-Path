04

Back to the valley

What would move into the game, and what it would cost.

  • Keep the patterns. The game’s koi hide painter is good, and this lab barely changes it. The body wave is the upgrade.
  • Swim in the shader. The tail pivots in JS today, 18 of them. A per-instance vertex wave removes that CPU work and lets the koi go up to a few hundred if wanted.
  • Boids are local. An O(n²) sweep is fine at thirty-odd. If the river ever holds hundreds, use a grid hash or move to the GPU.
  • Butterflies at two distances. Keep the Points cloud for the distance and swap to hinged wings within a few metres.
