Build a machiya
post by post

A Kyoto townhouse is a lattice, a seven-coat clay wall and a wave of fired tiles, all standing on one stone per post. This lab rebuilds it nine ways — from the flat boxes the game uses now to a cel-shaded, weathered street — and lets Michi climb the kōshi, run up the inuyarai and leap roof to roof. Everything is painted at load time.

How the winner moves into the valley

  • The post-by-post builder replaces the ~140 box() parts in buildMachiyaTeaHouse, buildMachiyaResidence, buildSecretMachiya and buildVillageHouse — one shared kit, four houses.
  • Instanced kawara becomes one draw call per roof instead of two curved slabs; the koushi becomes one InstancedMesh instead of N boxes.
  • The hybrid shader (painterly PBR textures + a soft toon ramp) is the target look, so the photoreal background and the painterly cat finally share one grade.
  • Weathering rides one per-house seed, so a repeated house kit reads as an old, lived-in street.
  • The climb & jump surfaces reuse the game's exact 7.2 m/s take-off and −20 m/s² gravity, so a gap that works here works in the valley.
