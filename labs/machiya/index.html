04

Back to the valley.

A kit to review before it becomes part of the game.

  • One closed roof, one shared profile. The supporting shell, tile rows and cat collision use the same roof height function. Tile overlap is deliberate; loose half-cylinders never serve as the whole roof.
  • Detail where the camera can see it. Instanced tiles, slats and boards for close houses; a textured shell for far houses. The measured draw and triangle counts are shown on every specimen.
  • Small textures, real light. Reuse the game’s procedural 256 px material sets. Save extra geometry for eaves, openings and silhouettes.
  • A lab first. The live game builders remain our reference. Promotion still needs material registration in MAT, distant LODs, collider review and device benchmarks. The lab’s climbing and wander controls are experiments.
