04

Back to the valley

What would move into the game, and what it would cost.

  • Wind the yarn once. Only three or four baked yarn geometries are needed, instanced with colours. A wound ball is a few thousand triangles, which is fine for ten of them.
  • Fuzz only when close. Shell fuzz is twelve draws a ball. Use it for the one yarn within a metre or two of the cat.
  • Glints are almost free. A single sprite per collectible, doing almost nothing most of the time, and the timing does the work.
  • Keep the emissive for the night. The old yarn’s emissive is what keeps it readable after dark. Sheen alone isn’t enough once the sun has gone.
