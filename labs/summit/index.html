Back to the valley

What would move into the game, and what it would cost.

  • This one is for the summit team. Nothing here touches mountaintop.js. These are suggestions for them to take or leave.
  • A window wave costs one uniform. The onset is stored per point once. Lighting the whole city in a wave is a single number moving.
  • Separate the layers. One haze card per distance band is cheap, and it reads more like a painting than exponential fog does.
  • Far means flat. Past about 400 m a painted strip costs less than the instancing and looks the same.
