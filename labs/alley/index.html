04

Back to the valley

What would move into the game, and what it would cost.

  • Keep the climb route. The crates’ collision and platform heights are the route to the tea house roof. Any new crate has to keep those exactly.
  • Bake the small stuff. Most of these props never move. Merging each kind into one static mesh keeps the street’s draw count close to where it is.
  • Respect the seeded build. New countryside props must not call random() mid-build, because the valley layout depends on the sequence. Give each a seeded generator of its own.
  • Colliders where the cat can reach. Nothing here needs more than a small circle or box, and the crates and barrels already have one each.
