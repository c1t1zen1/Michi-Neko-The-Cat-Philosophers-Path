04

Back to the valley

What would move into the game, and what it would cost.

  • Wetness is one uniform. Every material already routes through a small number of shared uniforms. A global uWet and uSnow would light up the whole valley at once.
  • Land the particles. Spawn a ring at the ground for a rain drop instead of letting the streak disappear. That is most of the realism here.
  • Keep the emitter centred. The game moves its particle box with the cat. A painted cell is a nice toy for this lab, but the game needs the box.
  • Petals cost nothing to keep. A landed petal is one instance. Reuse the old rest list once it’s full.
