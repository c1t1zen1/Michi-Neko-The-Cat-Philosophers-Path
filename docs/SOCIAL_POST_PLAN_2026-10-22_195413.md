## Posting order

### Act 1 — Why this exists

1. **Origin: the honeymoon, the cat, the Kyoto valley.**
   - Angle: split-screen of a real Kyoto photo against the matching spot in the game, over the 8s teaser (`docs/images/michi-neko-teaser-8s-v3.mp4`).
   - Hook: "I vibe-coded a Ghibli-style cat game from a honeymoon memory."
2. **"The whole game has no asset downloads."**
   - Angle: file-size counter beside the world loading in. Everything is painted at load time from 256px procedural textures.
   - A surprising, shareable claim.
3. **Built on a potato.**
   - Angle: the Core m3 laptop as the underdog. Show `&perf=1` timings and the Low / Medium / High quality toggles.
   - Honest constraint story; devs like it.

### Act 2 — The world comes alive

4. **Cobblestone before/after.** (`docs/images/cobble-before-after.png` already exists.)
   - Angle: 6-second wipe. Easy warm-up post.
5. ★ **Trees and bushes.**
   - Angle: the same tree built two ways — individual leaf sprays on twigs inside the play field, painted masses at the rim. Add the `&wind=4` gust test and the cat brushing through a bush so it rustles.
   - Hook: "Every leaf is placed by code."
6. ★ **Water overhaul.**
   - Angle: old vs new water in the same shot, then the cat paddling / koi swimming. A 10–15s captionless loop.
   - *Open item: list the key water upgrades so the post can name them.*
7. **The 60-minute day.**
   - Angle: 24-hour timelapse compressed to ~15s, with rain, snow and mist cutting in. Sets up the golden-hour and lantern shots.

### Act 3 — Life in the valley

8. ✅ **Cat anatomy glow-up.** The "meet the cat" beat.
9. ★ **Gameplay: what do you actually do?**
   - Angle: 60-second single take, no commentary. Shows no fail states, no timers, scent trails, NPC cats, quests.
   - Overlay: "No combat. No timer. No fail state."
   - The main pitch and the highest priority for reach.
10. **Sound: the valley has a soundtrack.**
    - Angle: mute/unmute the same scene to show how much audio carries. Music shifts with time of day and weather. Use Wave Lab visuals and the monk bowl. Good "ASMR" post.

### Act 4 — How it gets made

11. ✅ **Workshop carousel** (cadJS + dawCAT).
12. **I built my own DAW so an AI could compose the music.**
    - Angle: screen-record the agent panel streaming a composition, then play the result in-game. One story with a payoff.
13. **Bug reel.**
    - Angle: funny clips of past bugs — Luna hovering, the tilted collar, the black screen on Medium quality. Bloopers feel human.

### Act 5 — The payoff

14. ★ **Hilltop meditation stage.**
    - Angle: slow, quiet climb to the summit. Cut to the Kyoto vista, monk choir and bowl, wind, the cat falling asleep.
    - Long calm clip; closing caption like "the whole game was building to this." Use the "secret garden" framing as a teaser.

### Act 6 — What's next

15. **Roadmap tease: Kyoto → Tokyo.**
    - Angle: text-and-concept-art slides on the bullet-train sequence, parade float and forest chase (`FUTURE_QUESTS.md`). Poll: "what should I build first?"
16. **Play it now / feedback ask.**
    - Angle: a clip of someone's first 60 seconds, the play link, and one specific question for viewers.
