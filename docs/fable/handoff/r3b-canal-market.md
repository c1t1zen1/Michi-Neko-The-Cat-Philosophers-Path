## 2. For r5 (boatman)
The takasebune kit piece returns the boat record `{ group, plat, d, boatman, pole, wake, bowRing, lastS, dir }` in `dynamic: { boats, boat }` with `live: [group]`, exactly the classic shape. Attach node: `sockets.attach_boatman` is the boat-local group at the classic boatman's place (1.55, 0.28, -0.24); `world.update()` rotates it (y flip with direction, z tilt) so put the boatman inside it. `sockets.attach_pole` (child of it, at 0.04, 0.66, 0) is the pole pivot the update swings (rotation.z). Until r5 lands (R75) the kit boat carries the CLASSIC boatman and pole inside these two groups (same meshes, sizes and places as pieces/boat.js: legs, body, head, kasa, bamboo pole), so a swapped boat poles exactly as classic. r5 replaces that figure by emptying/replacing the children of `attach_boatman` (keep `attach_pole` as the pole pivot).

## 4. Glass and water
The canopy vault uses ctx.MAT.glass, so the facade's own glass pass (build.js) still moves it to the transparent batch. The canal's water/foam ribbons are `M.water`/`M.foam` in the static batch of the kit group (classic added them through `ctx.addGlass`, a separate transparent group): they sort as transparent either way; judged by eye in the L6 pairs.
