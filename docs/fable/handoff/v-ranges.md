## Reusable kit builders (s3-vista summit horizon)
Import with the current tag. All are deterministic and free of Countryside.
- `src/kit/ranges/landform.js`
  - `ridgeRingGeometry({ r0, r1, around, rings, grade, heightAt, colorAt, floorHalf, floorDrop, cacheKey })`: a polar height-field ring, graded radius (fine near r0), wrapped seam, normals before colour; `colorAt(x, z, h, ny, d, out)`. Pass any height function (summit ridges) and palette; `cacheKey` memoises deterministic callers.
  - `valleyRangeColors({ forest, strata, haze })`: the palette (forest/broadleaf/cedar stands, slope and altitude rock with strata, lowland opening, baked haze). `terrainHeight`, `terrainSector`, `sstep` are the valley field.
- `src/kit/ranges/forest.js`
  - `canopySet(tier, { fill, warmth })`: the four painted-mass crown variants (cached, never dispose); `flaredTrunk({ radial, rows, flare, seed })`; `FOREST_GREENS`, `CROWN_TIER`.
  - `buildEdgeForest`, `buildEdgeUndergrowth`, `buildFoothillForest` take (ctx, layout, params) with the classic layout shapes; a rim forest of any radius works by giving `trees` / `assign`.
- `src/kit/ranges/mist.js`: `mistTint(hour, out)` (cream by day, peach at dusk, blue-grey at night), `buildMistRing`, `buildDawnMist` on any `{ sprites: [{x,y,z,scale}] }`.
- `src/kit/ranges/village.js`: `buildDistantVillage` on any `{ houses: [{x,y,z,s,wall,roof,windows,ry}] }`.

## Tiers
The game builds mountains / foothill-forest / distant-village at tier far (band far) and edge-forest / edge-undergrowth / mist-ring at mid on high quality, far on medium (TIER_POLICY). So the far tier of the far-band assets carries the improvements (gabled roofs, tone gradient, slope-painted rock); edge-forest hero and mid are the classic crown recipe (the wall must stay full), far spends two tufts a clump.
