# 🐾 Cat Walk — Quick Start & Extended Gameplay Guide

> **猫の散歩** — A cat's stroll through a Kyoto-inspired valley.  
> This guide covers everything from your first steps to discovering every secret.

---

## Getting Started

### First Launch

1. Start the game (see [INSTALL.md](INSTALL.md) if you haven't already)
2. You'll see the **title screen** with an animated paw and cinematic orbit camera
3. Click **"New Game"** to begin (or **"Continue"** if you have a saved game)
4. Your tabby cat will appear in the valley at dawn — ready to explore

### The First 60 Seconds

- **Move around** with `W` `A` `S` `D` (or the joystick on mobile)
- **Look around** by dragging the mouse (or swiping the right side on touch)
- **Jump** with `Space` (or the JUMP button)
- Walk toward the cat ahead of you — that's **Luna**, and she has a quest for you

## The HUD — Japanese Parchment Scroll

Your heads-up display is styled as a **Japanese emakimono (parchment scroll)** in the top-left corner. Click the **📜 Menu** button to collapse or expand it.

### What It Shows

- **Yarn count** — how many yarn balls you've collected
- **Rank** — your current cat rank (Kitten, Curious Cat, etc.)
- **XP bar** — progress toward your next rank-up
- **Quest card** — your active quest with a progress bar
- **Time & weather** — current in-game time (HH:MM) and weather state
- **Inventory badges** — shows when you have the 🔑 Antique Key or 🪶 Guardian's Feather
- **Hints toggle** — turn the control hint text on/off

### Compass

At the top center of the screen, a **compass strip** shows cardinal directions (N, S, E, W) and icons for nearby points of interest:
- 🐱 Luna
- 🍡 Mochi
- ⚫ Kuro
- 🏮 Tea House
- 🎋 Bamboo Grove
- 🔑 Antique Key (when available)
- 🪶 Guardian's Feather (when available)

### Objective Markers

When you have an active objective, a floating icon with a distance label appears on screen. If the objective is off-screen, a golden **edge arrow** points you in the right direction.

## Core Gameplay Loop

```
Explore → Discover → Interact → Collect → Progress → Repeat
```

1. **Explore** the valley — follow paths, climb rooftops, wade into the river
2. **Discover** yarn balls, NPCs, hidden items, and secret areas
3. **Interact** with the world — talk to cats, ring the shrine bell, paw at koi
4. **Collect** yarn balls for XP and quest completion
5. **Progress** through ranks that unlock sprint, jump boost, and speed
6. **Repeat** — the valley changes with time of day and weather, so revisiting areas at different times reveals new moods and details

## NPCs — The Cats of the Valley

### Luna (月) — The Moonlight Cat
- **Location:** Near the center of the valley (by the spawn point)
- **Fur:** Dark charcoal with moonlit amber eyes and indigo silk ribbon
- **Personality:** Mysterious, wise, speaks in riddles about the valley's secrets
- **Quest:** Gives you **"Luna's Yarn Hunt"** — collect 3 yarn balls
- **Tip:** Approach her to trigger automatic dialogue on first meeting. Press E to greet her again anytime.

### Mochi (🍡) — The Bamboo Grove Cat
- **Location:** East side of the valley near the bamboo grove
- **Fur:** Warm golden-orange with cream belly
- **Personality:** Cheerful, chatty, loves bamboo whispers
- **Hint:** "They say a hidden house appears for cats who explore..."
- **Tip:** He mentions seeing something shiny near the old torii gate — this is a clue about the Antique Key

### Kuro (⚫) — The River Guardian
- **Location:** North side of the valley near the river
- **Fur:** Jet black with dark golden eyes
- **Personality:** Quiet, observant, speaks in short sentences
- **Hint:** "Follow the red shrine gates when the mist rolls in"
- **Tip:** Kuro walks slowly and stays near the water. Approach quietly (try prowl mode with C)

## The Secret Tea House — Walkthrough

The tea house is the game's main discovery quest. Here's how to find it:

### Step 1: Meet Luna
Walk forward from your spawn point. Luna will greet you automatically and start the "Luna's Yarn Hunt" quest.

### Step 2: Collect 3 Yarn Balls
Explore the valley — yarn balls glow with a pink emissive light and are scattered along paths, near buildings, and in hidden corners. Collect 3 to complete Luna's quest.

### Step 3: Find the Antique Key
Talk to **Mochi** near the bamboo grove (east side). He hints at something shiny near the torii gate. Head to the **bamboo shrine area** (look for the 🎋 icon on your compass). The Antique Key is hidden there — walk near it and press E to pick it up.

### Step 4: Unlock the Tea House
Follow the 🏮 icon on your compass to the machiya townhouse with the locked door. Press E while holding the key to unlock it. A **sliding shoji door transition** will play as you enter.

### Step 5: Explore the Interior
You're now inside **Hisomu-an (秘奥庵) Tea House** — a fully modeled Japanese room:
- **Tatami mat floor** (6-mat arrangement)
- **Tokonoma alcove** with hanging scroll (kakejiku) and bonsai pine
- **Chabudai table** with ceramic tea set and grilled sea bream
- **Red zabuton cushion** for napping
- **Paper andon lantern** with warm glow
- **Engawa veranda** overlooking a private zen rock garden with stone toro lantern

### Step 6: Interact Inside
- **Eat the fish** for a 40-second speed boost
- **Take a cozy nap** to advance time by 4 hours (great for seeing sunset or night)
- **Exit** through the front door to return to the valley

## The Guardian's Feather — Rooftop Climb

The bird's nest is hidden on a rooftop — the highest reachable point in the valley.

1. Look for **climbable platforms** — low ledges, roof edges, and elevated surfaces you can jump onto
2. Use **jump buffering** — press Space slightly before landing to chain jumps
3. The nest contains three speckled eggs and a **golden Guardian's Feather**
4. Press E to inspect and claim the feather trophy (+60 XP)
5. The feather appears in your HUD inventory badge

> **Tip:** Unlock the **Jump Boost** rank (80 XP) first — it makes rooftop climbing significantly easier.

## Day/Night Cycle & Weather

### Time of Day

The game runs a compressed 24-hour cycle that completes in **60 real minutes**. Time advances automatically, but you can also jump forward by **taking a cozy nap** in the tea house (+4 hours).

| Time | Lighting Mood | Best For |
|------|---------------|----------|
| **Dawn (5:30–7:30)** | Soft pinks, gentle warmth | Peaceful exploration, screenshots |
| **Day (7:30–17:00)** | Bright, warm, energetic | General exploration, NPC interactions |
| **Golden Hour (17:00–19:00)** | Amber, long shadows, romantic | Photography, atmospheric moments |
| **Sunset (19:00–19:30)** | Deep orange to purple | Most beautiful lighting in the game |
| **Night (19:30–5:30)** | Cool blue, moonlight, lantern glow | Mystery, quiet wandering, fireflies |

### Weather States

Weather changes dynamically every 5–10 minutes:

| Weather | Visual Effect | Gameplay Effect |
|---------|---------------|-----------------|
| **Clear** | Default bright sky | Normal visibility |
| **Cloudy** | Overcast, softer light | Reduced shadow contrast |
| **Rain** | Rain particles, darker sky, wet look | Closer fog, ambient rain sound |
| **Snow** | Snow particles, bright cool tones | Shorter visibility, peaceful mood |
| **Mist** | Dense fog, mysterious atmosphere | Very limited visibility, atmospheric |

> **Tip:** Weather transitions take about 2.5 seconds to blend. The lighting, fog, sky colors, and ambient audio all crossfade smoothly.

## Advanced Movement

### Jump Techniques

- **Short hop:** Tap Space quickly and release — the cat does a small hop
- **Full jump:** Hold Space — the cat jumps to full height
- **Variable height:** Release Space early to cut the jump short (the cat decelerates)
- **Coyote time:** You can still jump for ~0.12 seconds after walking off a ledge
- **Jump buffering:** Press Space slightly before landing — the jump triggers the moment you touch ground

### Sprint Mechanics

- Sprint unlocks at **Curious Cat** rank (30 XP — roughly 3 yarn balls)
- Hold **Shift** on desktop or push the joystick to the **full edge** on mobile
- Sprinting increases your FOV slightly for a sense of speed
- On mobile, the joystick's raw input magnitude determines walk vs. sprint

### Prowl Mode

- Press **C** to toggle prowl stance
- The cat crouches low and moves at 45% speed
- Purr audio plays while prowling
- Useful for approaching Kuro quietly or for atmospheric slow exploration

### Water Interaction

- Walking into the river slows you to 55% speed
- Entering water creates a **splash effect** with droplet particles
- Moving in water spawns **ripple rings** on the surface
- Gravity is reduced in water (buoyancy effect)
- You can paw at koi fish and drink fresh water

## Tips for New Players

1. **Talk to every cat** — each NPC gives XP and hints about secrets
2. **Follow the compass icons** — they point to NPCs, the tea house, and key items
3. **Collect yarn early** — 3 yarn balls completes Luna's quest and gets you 30+ XP toward sprint unlock
4. **Explore at different times** — the valley looks completely different at sunset vs. dawn vs. night
5. **Try photo mode during golden hour** — the bloom and warm lighting make stunning screenshots
6. **Climb everything** — rooftops hide the bird's nest and offer beautiful vantage points
7. **Eat the fish before exploring** — the 35% speed boost lasts 40 seconds and helps you cover ground
8. **Nap to change the time** — the cozy nap in the tea house advances 4 hours, perfect for catching sunset
9. **Listen to the music** — the generative soundtrack changes with time of day; night brings sparse, contemplative plucks
10. **Take your time** — there's no fail state, no timer, no pressure. This is a game about wandering

## Save System

- The game **auto-saves every 12 seconds** during gameplay
- Saves are stored in your browser's `localStorage`
- Your position, yarn count, XP, rank, quest progress, collected items, world state (key/tea house/feather), time of day, and weather are all saved
- Click **"Save & Quit to Title"** in the pause menu to save and return to the title screen
- Click **"Continue"** on the title screen to resume from your last save
- **New Game** clears your save and starts fresh

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I'm stuck and can't find anything to do
Follow the compass icons at the top of the screen. The 🐱 icon points to Luna (who gives you a quest). The 🎋 icon points to the bamboo grove (where the key is hidden). The 🏮 icon points to the tea house door.

### How do I sprint?
Sprint unlocks at **Curious Cat** rank (30 XP). Collect 3 yarn balls and talk to Luna to reach this rank quickly.

### Can I play offline?
Once the page is loaded, the game runs entirely client-side. However, Three.js is loaded from a CDN, so you need internet on first load. Offline caching via PWA is planned for a future release.

### Is there combat?
No. Cat Walk is an exploration game with no combat, no enemies, and no fail states. The focus is on discovery, atmosphere, and relaxation.

### How long is the game?
The current alpha content can be explored thoroughly in 30–60 minutes. The secret tea house quest takes about 15–20 minutes if you know where to go. Future updates will add more zones, NPCs, and story content.

### Can I customize my cat?
Not yet — the player cat is a fixed brown tabby with a red ribbon and golden bell. Customization (fur colors, collars, accessories) is planned for future updates.

### Why does the game look different at different times?
The dynamic day/night cycle and weather system continuously change the lighting, sky colors, fog, and ambient audio. The same location can look dramatically different at dawn vs. sunset vs. night in the rain.
