旅のしおり / THE TRAVELLING SKETCHBOOK

Little things, along the way.

A postcard at each brass seal. A country through the windows. Take your time.

The route & controls

    WASD / arrows to move · Shift to run · Space to jump · C or Ctrl to duck · E to use a hatch, greet a friend or take a postcard · drag to orbit · wheel to zoom · Q to recenter · F to settle in and ride to the next view · R to return to a safe roof · M for sound · Esc to pause. Controller: left stick, A jump, B crouch, X use, right stick look.

    The train keeps your place while paused. Riding to the next view is optional and keeps your postcards and exploration.
